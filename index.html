About Summer

Why select Summer?

Summer brings real-world hospitality industry experience, combined with humor and storytelling to her keynotes. Her goals are to:

  • Help leaders at all levels build purpose
  • Ignite action
  • Inspire teams
  • Stay centered in the face of adversity

She is the creator of the M.A.G.I.C. model for leadership, a practical framework addressing:

  • Self-management
  • Purpose alignment
  • Team galvanization
  • Team inspiration
  • Chaos control

Key topics

  • The M.A.G.I.C. Leadership Model
  • Neurodivergence in the Workplace
  • Quality Leaders = Quality Culture

Author and coach

She is a proud neurodivergent leadership expert and PhD researcher who shares straightforward leadership truths to empower audiences and organizations to chart new paths forward. Additionally, she’s a best-selling author.

Summer provides values-based coaching that is rooted in co-creating. She understands that there are no one-size-fits-all approaches to human development. This means she works with clients on unpacking ideas and discovering what’s next. She creates tools for each individual client and creates a judgment-free zone for self-discovery.
